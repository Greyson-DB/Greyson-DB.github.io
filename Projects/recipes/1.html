Venison Breakfast

My grandfather is a hunter so i would have this a lot for breakfast over the summer.

Ingredients Measurements
Venison 5 oz preferably
Eggs 2 per person
Bacon As many peices as you would like
Butter Scrape a litte from the stick

Venison

  1. First Get out a pan and start melting the butter on medium heat
  2. Next cut your venison into thin pancake like slices
  3. Once your butter is melted on to the pan put the venison in the pan
  4. Put on medium heat
  5. The blood will rise to the top of the venison, once you see theat happen flip the peices
  6. Repeat this process until you stop seeing blood rise to the top, This means it has been fully cooked

Eggs

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl or directly into a pan, depending on the preferred technique.
  2. Add a splash of water or dairy if using, and season with salt
  3. Melt butter in a non-stick skillet over medium to medium heat
  4. Pour in the eggs and gently stir or push with a spatula, allowing curds to form slowly
  5. Remove the eggs from the heat while they still look glossy

Bacon

  • Put the bacon on a large, paper lined, rimmed sheet pan close together, but not touching.
  • Bake at 400°F for 12 to 15 minutes
  • Take out and let it cool for 1-2 minutes
