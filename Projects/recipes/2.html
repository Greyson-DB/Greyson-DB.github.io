Lobster Dinner

My grandfather is a lobsterman along with being a hunter so almost evey day he would wake up at 4 am and go to work on the boat.

Ingredients Measurements
Lobster 1 alive lobster per person
Corn 2 or 3 cobbs per person
Butter 1/4 stick per person

Lobster

  1. Take your live lobsters out of your tank
  2. Boil of medium heat for 1 hour and 30 minutes
  3. Take out and enjoy

Corn

  1. Shuck the corn if not already done
  2. Add the corn to boiling water, turn off the heat, cover the pot, and let it steep for 10 to 12 minutes

Butter

  1. Melt butter until entirly liquid
  2. Puor into a little bowl/cup

Eating

  • You are going to want to crack a claws open with a nut cracker
  • You then want to use a needle fork to get the meat
  • Then dip in butter and eat
